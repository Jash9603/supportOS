# SupportOS - Help Center & Knowledge Base

## What is SupportOS?

SupportOS is an AI-powered customer support platform for SaaS companies and online businesses. It provides a chat widget you embed on your website that instantly answers customer questions using your company's documentation. When the AI can't handle a question, it automatically escalates to your human support team with full context.

---

## Getting Started

### How do I sign up?
Visit our website and click "Start for free." You'll get a free trial with your first 500 tickets included - no credit card required.

### How do I set up my account?
1. Sign up and log into the dashboard
2. Go to **Chatbot** → Upload your company documents (FAQs, product guides, PDFs)
3. Go to **Settings** → Copy the embed code
4. Paste the embed code into your website's HTML
5. That's it! Your AI support agent is live

### What file formats can I upload?
You can upload PDF files, text documents, and other common document formats. The AI will learn from whatever you upload and use that knowledge to answer customer questions.

## Dashboard & Features

### Overview Page
The Overview page shows your key performance indicators at a glance:
- **Total Tickets** - Number of support tickets in the last 7 days
- **Bot Resolved** - Tickets the AI handled without human help
- **Avg Response Time** - How quickly customers get answers
- **Customer Satisfaction** - Based on interaction quality

### Inbox
The Inbox is where you manage all customer conversations:
- **All Open** - View all unresolved tickets
- **Assigned to me** - Tickets assigned to you specifically
- **Resolved** - Previously closed tickets
- Click any ticket to see the full conversation thread
- Type a reply and click **Send** or use **Resolve & Send** to close the ticket

### Analytics
Deep analytics to understand your support operations:
- **Customer Satisfaction Trend** - Track happiness over time
- **Top Customer Questions** - AI-clustered by topic to see what customers ask most
- **Resolution Breakdown** - Bot vs human resolution rates
- **Peak Support Hours** - When customers need help most
- **Escalation Rate** - Percentage of tickets needing human intervention
- **Response Time Comparison** - Bot vs human speed
- You can export analytics as a PDF report

### Chatbot Configuration
Train your AI assistant:
- Upload documents to your **Knowledge Base**
- The AI learns from your documents and answers questions based on them
- Test the chatbot directly from the dashboard before going live
- Documents can be added or removed at any time

### Settings
- **Profile** - Update your name
- **Embed Code** - Copy the JavaScript snippet to add the chat widget to your website
- Simply paste the code before the closing `</body>` tag on your site

## Chat Widget

### How do I install the widget on my website?
1. Go to **Settings** in your dashboard
2. Copy the embed code snippet
3. Paste it into your website's HTML, just before the closing `</body>` tag
4. The chat bubble will appear on your site automatically

### What does the widget look like?
A small chat bubble appears in the bottom-right corner of your website. When customers click it, a chat window opens where they can type their questions and get instant AI-powered responses.

### Can I customize the widget?
The widget automatically uses your organization name and provides a clean, professional chat interface that works on both desktop and mobile devices.

## Troubleshooting

### The widget isn't showing on my website
- Make sure the embed code is placed before the closing `</body>` tag
- Check that your website doesn't have a Content Security Policy blocking external scripts
- Try clearing your browser cache and refreshing the page

### The AI isn't answering questions correctly
- Make sure you've uploaded relevant documents to the Knowledge Base
- The AI answers based on your uploaded docs - the more comprehensive they are, the better the answers
- Try uploading additional FAQs or product documentation

### I'm not receiving ticket notifications
- Check your Inbox in the dashboard - all tickets appear there in real time
- Make sure you're checking the correct filter (All Open vs Assigned to me)

### Customers say the chat isn't working
- Verify the embed code is correctly installed on your website
- Check that the widget script URL is accessible
- Try testing from an incognito/private browser window
